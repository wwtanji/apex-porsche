1963–1998

Air-cooled

For thirty-five years the 911 cooled its engine with air alone. The last air-cooled 911 left the line in 1998, and collectors have been chasing them ever since.

Heritage

  1. 1948

    The first car to carry the Porsche name, the 356 “No. 1” roadster, is registered in Gmünd, Austria.

  2. 1963

    The 901 is shown at the Frankfurt motor show. Within a year it is renamed, and the 911 begins.

  3. 1974

    The 911 Turbo arrives at the Paris motor show and turns the whale tail into an icon.

  4. 1998

    The last air-cooled 911 is built. Its successor is cooled by water.

  5. 2019

    The Taycan launches as Porsche’s first fully electric car.
